## 2c. Verification card — how it works since v1.1.12, limits, open points
**Rule of the design: a stop is inferred only where something was logged; the heart rate only times it; where the picture has more than one reading the end is shown but nothing is concluded from it.** Code: `analysis.js` section "Where the running stopped" (`runTimeline` → `{ start, end, how, sure, why, bouts, stops, samples, lagMs, tailSec }`, thresholds in `RUN_END`), consumers in `prescribe.js`, card in `app.js` (`#vc-runend`, `#vc-runend-ok`).
- **What marks a stop**: a lactate entry (the time Save was pressed; a skipped entry counts too), a Pause the heart rate confirms (an unconfirmed Pause is ignored — touched by accident, or never released), the rep clock of an LT2 session, `session.runEndSec` (typed or confirmed in the card). `how` = `sample | pause | manual | phase | finish | hr`.
- **Timing by heart rate** (`stopBefore`): on a 5-s grid, the latest moment within 12 min before the entry at which the heart rate was still at its level (median of the 2 min before, not sinking) and after which it fell ≥ 10 bpm, the following minute ≥ 8 bpm lower. Walked first, then stood (staircase): the first step is the end; steps more than 3 min apart → uncertain. A surge at the end after which the running went on at the old level is not a stop. Two entries after one stop are two fingers unless the heart rate came up ≥ 15 bpm in between *and* got back to within 8 bpm of the level it stopped from (or was still climbing after under 90 s) — running resumed → the first is the 10-min sample; a walk or a cool-down jog is not running. A stop that was never back at its level before the next one: the running in between counts from where the heart rate reached the next stop's level (a lower speed after the 10-min sample). The last stop, never back at its level, but the heart rate then held within 16 bpm of it for 10 min after the values were typed: the running went on — not the end.
- **Sample roles**: ≤ 4 min from the start (LT2: before the first work phase) = rest; typed after the final stop = end (also when the *time* of that stop is not relied on — only an end estimated from the heart rate alone files nothing); a stop that was followed by more running = 10-min; with no stop of its own, by the clock (last 5 min / 15 %). A field typed in the card (`session.lactateChecks`, with `lactateChecksV: 2`) is final for that field, also when emptied. Records edited in the v1.1.11 card stored all three fields; there a field equal to v1.1.11's clock filing counts as untouched (`typedChecks`). The card takes 0.3–25 mmol/L and a decimal comma.
- **Uncertain (`sure = false`, `why`)**: `no-entry` (no stop could be tied to anything logged near the end and the recording does not end at the running level → estimate from heart rate alone; also when drops were seen that ended nothing and the recording ends ≥ 10 bpm below the level they left from, or ends ≥ 10 bpm below the heart rate before the last value typed), `late-entry` (a value typed, or Pause pressed, > 6 min after the stop; a skipped dialog does not count), `two-steps`, `level` (stop found only at a level ≥ 8 bpm below the run, or below a level held in the 12 min before it), `shallow` (typed or Pause pressed ≥ 2 min after the stop and the heart rate never got 25 bpm down), `error` (the code failed on the record → plain recording). Then: no heart rate in a zone change (speed part only), no end-only MLSS proxy verdict, session metrics as before, multi-day point without heart rate (`?`; its heart rate is read only between certain runs on either side, never copied beyond them — otherwise the threshold gets no heart rate and 「적용」 stays disabled), the SmO2 line marked as an estimated window; a value typed at a stop that something logged keeps its role (end, or 10-min if the running went on), a value without a stop of its own goes by the clock. 「확정」 or a typed time stores `runEndSec` and settles it.
- **Stops inside the run** (10-min sample): `[from, back at level, lowest point]`; left out of run time, heart-rate windows and drift windows (the 8–13-min window slides past a stop); α1 is dropped from 15 s before a heart-rate-timed stop until one α1 window (2 min) after the level is regained. A last-5-min heart rate or a drift figure needs at least a minute of heart rate in its window (strap taken off before Finish → no figure rather than one made of a few seconds).
- **Heart-rate data**: one value a second (live) or one per beat (replay) is used as it is; a file with a value only every few seconds (watch on smart recording) is filled in to one a second, gaps over a minute stay gaps; samples out of order are sorted.
- **Unchanged on purpose**: a session with nothing logged that ends while running gives exactly the figures of v1.1.11 (differential test in `test_run_end`) — except that a figure which rested on less than a minute of heart rate is no longer given; step tests are not touched.
- **Known limits**: (a) a cool-down less than ≈ 8–10 bpm below the run cannot be told from the run — set the end by hand; (b) "kick, jog at the earlier level, then sample" and "surge, run on, sample" are the same picture: when the stretch at the earlier level lasted about 2.5 min or more the end is the final stop, when it was shorter the end is where the kick / surge ended — in both cases called certain (a kick with a slow recovery and Finish minutes later is flagged instead); (c) a value typed within 2 min of a ≥ 10-bpm drop is taken as typed at a stop, so an eased finish counts as the end of the run; (d) nothing logged + a permanent pace reduction earlier in the run → flagged estimate; (e) running taken up again for less than about a minute after a stop (heart rate up less than 15 bpm) is not seen as running: a second value is then a second finger of the same stop — correct the fields by hand; the other way round, a cool-down jog that gets within 8 bpm of the run is taken for running (3rd review: 4 % of value–jog–value sessions called certain with the end at the second value); and a value typed at the stop followed by several minutes of jogging within ≈ 5 bpm of the run is filed as a 10-min value with the end only estimated; (f) a heart-rate-timed end lags the true stop: in the wide simulation of the second review (1 500 runs — recovery time constant 35–150 s, a plateau or small rise for up to 12 s after stopping, noise and slow wander up to 4 bpm, a value every 1–10 s) the median was +10 s, 95 % within +28 s, 0.7 % beyond ±45 s (extremes +65 / −105 s); the last-5-min heart rate of an end called certain was within ±0.4 bpm in 90 % and within ±1.7 bpm in all of these (α1 keeps 15 s clear of the end, so a late end lets some standing beats into its last values); third review (own two-compartment kinetics, 6 000 plain sessions): end median +13 s, p99 +33 s, 16 beyond ±45 s; HR error p1 −0.3 / p99 +0.5, max 3.6 bpm; easy runs at HR ≈ 110–115 with the value typed 2–4 min after the stop are flagged in 20–33 % (`shallow`); (g) a steady run whose heart rate wanders by ±10 bpm over minutes (slow wander with an sd of 3–4 bpm) can show a "stop" that was none: in that simulation 6 % of plain runs with such wander were flagged, under 1 % called certain with an end up to 2 min early; below 2 bpm of wander none.
- **Open**: (1) **validation on a real recording** — ask the owner for a backup JSON or session CSV of a run with a stop and a cool-down, replay it against `runTimeline`, keep the file out of the repo; (2) the step-test stage table still takes "the last 60 s" on the wall clock when Pause is pressed in mid-stage; (3) the thresholds in `RUN_END` were tuned on simulated kinetics (fast phase τ ≈ 15 s, slow 45–110 s). A heart rate that has not fallen 10 bpm by 90 s after the entry is not taken as a stop; the session is then handled like one with nothing logged (an estimate marked uncertain — or, when the heart rate stays within 8 bpm of the run, the end of the recording, which is limit (a)).
- Branch `wip/verification-card` (the first attempt, estimate from heart rate alone, rejected by two reviews) is superseded — delete it.
- **Bluetooth — left from the focused review of v1.1.11 (all low; reasoned or shown on a simulated strap, none seen on a device):** (1) the 20-s limit covers link-up and service discovery together: a link that comes up late in an attempt is reset once and comes back on the next attempt (normally 1–5 s later; a strap that needed ~20 s to link every time would never get through — give discovery its own limit once the link is up), and a first connection slower than 20 s now fails with TimeoutError where v1.1.10 kept waiting; (2) between timed-out attempts the status reads "connecting", and Connect is not disabled while a first connection is in flight (further taps are ignored); (3) a link can stay open without an owner only if two pending `gatt.connect()` calls on one device settle differently, which Chromium does not do; (4) switching the sensor kind and picking the same strap again within 1.5 s relies on Chromium running stop/start of notifications in order.
- **Also proposed, not done (ask the owner):** (1) a switch to turn α1 guidance off for this athlete (LT1-mode voice "알파1 낮음", the −3 bpm auto-adjust and the α1 sub-labels all assume α1 0.75 = LT1, which §3 says is wrong here); (2) store SmO2 at 1 Hz in sessions (Train.Red exports are 10 Hz → ~1 MB per session, and Home/Plan load every session); (3) "finish" an unfinished autosaved session; (4) artifact voice warning in test/free modes.

## 4. Solo protocol rules / 혼자 할 때 규칙
- Lactate technique: hands warm, washed with warm water and **fully dry (no alcohol residue, no sweat)**, wipe the first drop, no milking, fill the strip in one touch; end sample within 45 s of stopping; duplicate (two fingers) when a value looks odd (agree within 0.3). Keep caffeine/breakfast consistent on verification days. Strips: insert only when ready (2-min auto-off); meter ≥ 15 °C.
- **LT1 verification:** Free mode, speed entered, 35 min constant, cue "35"; type the value while standing (same rule as below). End ≤ 2.0 (and ≤ rest +1.0) → below; 2.1–2.5 → borderline; > 2.5 (or rise > 1.5) → above.
- **MLSS verification:** Free mode, **purpose = MLSS**, 30 min constant, warm-up outside the session, cue "30" (or "10,30" if a mid-run stop is possible; no need to pause the app — the stop is found from the entry). **Type each value while still standing, before walking or jogging on** — the entry is what marks the stop (§2c); after that the cool-down and a late 종료 no longer matter. Check the card's 「달리기 종료」 time afterwards. With a 10-min sample: rise ≤ 1.0 → at/below (next +0.3), > 1.0 → above (−0.4). End-only proxy: < 25 min → withheld; end < 3 → below; ≥ 6 → above; 3–6 with HR drift (min 8–13 → last 5) ≤ 6 bpm and SmO2 steady → likely at/below (+0.3); drift > 8 bpm, SmO2 still falling or RPE ≥ 18 → likely above (−0.3); 6–8 → repeat.
- **Zone updates:** verification card "존에 반영" (confirmed run raises a floor, failed run lowers a cap, from the run's own speed and the heart rate of its last 5 min of running; while the end of the run is uncertain only the speed part is offered); multi-day lactate curve (≥ 3 speeds, Analysis tab "적용"); manual thresholds override. Any constant-speed Free/LT1 session (or one-rep LT2 session) with speed + end lactate feeds the curve; interval sessions do not.
- LT2 **training** (not testing): LT2 session mode, 4×8 min (3-min easy) → 4×10 → 5×10 → recovery week → 3×15 → 2×20 → 30-min tempo, at MLSS speed, HR LT2 ±3; optional lactate after the last rep 3–4.5 = on target.
